# Product definition (draft)

## Audience and purpose
Citizens, students, job applicants, researchers, journalists and policy readers should be able to explore reservation rules and evidence across Indian jurisdictions without requiring prior legal expertise. Editorial neutrality, source traceability and temporal correctness are core product properties.

## Navigation
- Home: search-first entry and educational topics
- Rules: jurisdictions, official community list entries, education, employment and political representation
- Reach: observed representation, filled posts, backlog, institutional and year filters
- Effects: research questions, study cards, scope, limitations and evidence links
- Debates: argument/counterargument with separate empirical evidence and normative positions
- History: timelines of laws, government orders, commissions and court decisions
- Data & Tools: comparisons, tables, downloadable data, source viewer

## MVP (demonstration interface)
Responsive navigation; homepage; community explorer; Karnataka and Central jurisdiction views; side-by-side comparisons; evidence-card examples; timeline; search; admin interface shell.

All initial records are synthetic demonstration data, visually labelled on every relevant screen. Do not display hypothetical real caste classifications or invented Gazette citations as if factual.

## Content integrity
Separate (a) official source identity, (b) accurate extraction of source text, and (c) interpretation of effect. Show last verified date and status. Never imply that appearance on an official community list by itself means eligibility for every scheme, employer or institution.

## Out of scope for initial release
All-India verified caste coverage, definitive eligibility determination, large-scale OCR/crawling, paid AI integrations, individual case tracking and mass ingestion of government schemes.

## Success criteria
Excellent mobile usability, task-based discovery, easily understandable explanations, source transparency and maintainable, testable components.
